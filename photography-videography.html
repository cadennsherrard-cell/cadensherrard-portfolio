Behind the Camera

Photography & Videography

Capturing the moments that paid social alone can't create.

This work has taken shooting to a wide range of locations, from studio product setups through to filming abroad on tight turnarounds.

  1. Barcelona, Spain

    Sole videographer and photographer on a trip to Barcelona to film content for a campaign giving customers the chance to win an El Clásico trip - flying Manchester to Barcelona and back in a single day, with an itinerary built around multiple locations. Shot on a camera and gimbal, with a DJI Mic 3 wireless setup and receiver for audio.

  2. Köln, Germany

    Two trips to Cologne: the first to film a promotional ad for a private-jet trip to the 2024 Euros, the second to capture the trip itself once it was underway - the winners, the host, and the general atmosphere of a city in the grip of football fever. Shot on the same camera, gimbal and DJI Mic 3 wireless setup used in Barcelona.

  3. Stadiums

    Up and down the country with a presenter filming TikTok content built around fan interactions and trivia tailored to individual Premier League fanbases, from the Emirates in North London to St James' Park in Newcastle, and the Racecourse Ground in Wrexham - again on the camera, gimbal and DJI Mic 3 setup.

  4. Studio

    The most frequent setting: photographing competition prizes from multiple angles, then filming close-ups, mid shots, wide shots and pans - enough to show a shirt's texture, a signature clearly, or the product as a whole. Daily studio use means lighting, backdrops and props are second nature.
