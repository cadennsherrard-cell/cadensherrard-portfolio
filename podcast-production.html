Podcast Production

Recording, mixing, and cutting long-form audio and video from first take to final cut.

The Offsiders Podcast ran for 19 episodes, produced end to end solo: producer, camera operator, editor and publisher, plus cutting the promotional clips used to push each episode out on social.

Each episode started with a scan of that week's biggest football headlines, recorded on a multi-mic setup but shot on a single camera - the footage was then cropped in post to simulate a three-camera setup rather than running three cameras live. Editing meant lining up five audio tracks against the footage, cutting a rough pass, then refining it repeatedly until the edit held up. Once locked, each episode was scheduled to go live on Spotify and YouTube, followed by the short-form cut-down: a teaser for the next episode and a standalone short-form clip built around one of that week's conversations.
